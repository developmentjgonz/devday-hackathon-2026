# Rehearsal: opening paw

Gold: Stageshow's Loading example. This scene keeps its browser frame and responsive crop, but uses the real Mews loading indicator. The empty screen is intentional: the paw is the only visible content while the app opens.

Beats: the paw pulses in size and opacity on a 1.2-second loop while the app is opening. The stage holds for two cycles and has no cursor or scene change. Reduced motion shows the paw still.

Captured: after the size pulse was added, desktop and 390 px contact sheets and the full-screen fallback at 664 × 320, including reduced motion. Earlier checks of the opacity-only version covered a 390 px state sheet, a reduced-motion still, a slowed motion sheet, and light and dark themes.

Gate: the revised desktop and phone loops reported `rehearsal passed` with complete coverage. The earlier opacity-only slow-motion capture had inconclusive coverage, with four sampling gaps; it was not repeated after the size change.

Review: the original paw felt too small and neutral in a full-screen state. The revised 56 px shared size and brand tone give it more presence. A small scale pulse now makes loading more apparent while the paw stays centered. The phone crop was changed after the first gate reported an undersized browser window.

Not checked: a physical phone or an independent fresh-eye review.
